Built for what SIH26133 actually asks

The brief is specific: accessibility and quality of public healthcare, particularly in rural and underserved areas — not a general hospital app, access for the people the current system underserves. In rural Maharashtra, the closest hospital is often not the one with a free bed, a working ventilator, or the right blood type. And the real-time bed dashboards built in a hurry during COVID mostly went quiet within a year, because nothing rewarded a facility for keeping its numbers honest. MedGrid is built to fix both problems at once — capability over proximity, and trust over hope.

How it works

One request, four things happening underneath it.

  1. 1

    Describe the need

    Speak or type it — in Marathi, Hindi, or English. "ICU near Pune" becomes a structured request in under a second.

  2. 2

    Match capability, not distance

    Facilities are ranked by whether they actually have the bed, equipment, or blood type needed — then by how fast a patient can really get there.

  3. 3

    Rank by real-time trust

    Every ranking weighs how recently, and how reliably, a facility has been updating its own numbers.

  4. 4

    Reroute as it happens

    If the top match fills up mid-transit, the next best option is already known.

Data you can actually trust

A ranking is only as good as the numbers behind it. MedGrid watches every update a facility makes — a jump that doesn't make sense, like a facility with two ICU beds suddenly reporting five hundred free, gets flagged and de-ranked automatically, before a patient is ever routed toward it.

Built on infrastructure fast enough to matter

Every piece was chosen for one thing: speed a rural connection can still feel.

Groq

Near-instant language parsing, on dedicated inference hardware.

Sarvam AI

Voice and speech built for Indian languages, hosted in India.

Supabase

Live facility data over WebSockets — no polling, no lag.

Mapbox

Real drive-time to a facility, not straight-line distance.

Health Facility Registry

Facility identity aligned to India's own hospital registry.
